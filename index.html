Beyond the classroom

Step into the data-driven world of professional auto racing.

Forget cardboard bridges and generic coding camps. This program bridges the gap between a passion for cars, gaming, math, or physics and a high-value path into engineering.

Over four intensive weeks, students learn how race cars generate grip, how elite teams use simulation before track time, and how engineers turn telemetry into faster laps — then prove it in a simulated race weekend challenge.

4-Week Intensive

Curriculum Overview

Vehicle dynamics fundamentals, hands-on simulation work, and professional data tools — designed specifically for high school students.

  1. Week 1 | Race Vehicle Dynamics

    The Fundamentals

    Explore the core physics of race vehicles — mechanical grip, weight transfer, tire behavior, and aerodynamics — and how they dictate car behavior when pushed to the limit.

  2. Week 2 | Simulation

    Predicting Performance

    Discover how top-tier teams validate setups in virtual environments. Modify real parameters — spring rates, anti-roll bars, wing angles — and test the results.

  3. Week 3 | Performance, Telemetry & Data Analysis

    Reading the Squiggly Lines

    Analyze real data with professional tools like MoTeC i2 Pro. Interpret driver and vehicle outputs to see how cars respond to setup changes.

  4. Week 4 | Race Weekend Challenge

    The Ultimate Test

    Pair up as Driver and Race Engineer. Analyze live data, optimize the setup, and guide your driver to the fastest lap on the grid in a simulated race weekend.

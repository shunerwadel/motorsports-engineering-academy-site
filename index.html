Beyond classroom theory

Step into the world of professional engineering at the top level of motorsports.

Move beyond impractical theory and superficial bootcamps. This program combines relevant academics with hands-on experience in how engineering is actually done at the highest levels of motorsports.

Over four intensive weeks, students will learn and apply vehicle dynamics fundamentals inside simulation software, analyze telemetry just like a trackside engineer, and put it all together by developing setup adjustments during a mock race weekend competition.

4-Week Intensive

Curriculum Overview

Learning focuses on vehicle dynamics fundamentals, simulation tool developement, and professional data analysis techniques — designed specifically for high school students.

  1. Week 1 | Race Vehicle Dynamics

    The Fundamentals

    Explore the core physics of race vehicles — mechanical grip, weight transfer, tire behavior, aerodynamics — and how engineers develop the vehicle setup using these fundamentals to achieve maximum performance on track.

  2. Week 2 | Simulation

    Predicting Performance

    Discover how top-tier teams build sophisticated simulation software to validate setups in virtual environments. Help build a laptop based simulation tool by developing inputs to the vehicle model and adding performance metric ouputs.

  3. Week 3 | Performance, Telemetry & Data Analysis

    Reading the Squiggly Lines

    Learn how data is gathered from race vehicles while on track and translated into key understanding of vehicle perfromance and balance. Then, get hands-on generating data from a consumer grade simualtor and performing data analysis by building custom plots and visuals using professional data viewing software.

  4. Week 4 | Race Weekend Challenge

    The Ultimate Test

    Test your course knowledge by competing against fellow students in mock race weekend sessions - run simulations, analyze results, test your changes, get driver feedback, and develop the fastest setup.
